## 1. Logo rules
- The logo is an **M-shaped mark with three cyan connection nodes**, plus the wordmark "M R S" (Space Grotesk Bold, already converted to outlines). Every logo file is outlined paths only: no live text, fonts, images or scripts inside the SVG.
- Dark backgrounds use ink `#E6EDF7` and cyan `#22D3EE`. Light backgrounds use ink `#0B1220` and cyan `#0891B2`. One-colour print and watermarks use pure black or white.
- Minimum width: lockup 80 px; the mark alone 16 px; below 24 px use the favicon version. Clear space around the logo equals one node circle. Never recolour, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or place it on a busy photo.

## 4. Where the logo is used
Header, footer, loading screen, sign-in page, admin sidebar, 404 page; invoice, receipt and Project File PDFs (mono or on-light version in the header); every email (on-light lockup, 140 px wide, with alt text "M R S"); the public receipt-verify page; the QR codes (small mark at the centre only if error correction stays at level H and a scan test passes). ## 5. Camellia (the AI assistant)
- Rename the assistant to **Camellia** in the interface, system prompts, emails, receipts, admin, code comments, database labels and the page title. Search the whole project for the old name and replace it. Keep old stored chats readable.
- Camellia always says she is an AI assistant of M R S, never claims to be human, and signs messages `— Camellia, M R S AI assistant`.
- Her avatar is a pink camellia flower with a golden centre, in a circle: use `camellia-avatar-on-dark.svg` or `camellia-avatar-on-light.svg` from the kit, and `camellia-flower.svg` for the chat header and empty states. If those files are not uploaded, use a pink circle `#EC4899` with a white letter C until they are. Pink set: `#EC4899`, `#F472B6`, `#F9A8D4`, `#FBCFE8`, centre `#FBBF24`. The flower is Camellia's mark only, not the company logo.
- Voice: warm, calm, concise, professional; asks one or two questions at a time; confirms what she understood; tells the client what happens next. Not flirty, not pushy, no emoji walls (at most one, occasionally).
- Autonomy: she may collect the brief, do the work, prepare previews, draft messages and answer questions alone. She must ask the owner before refunds, discounts, deadlines she cannot meet, access to any account, or anything legal or medical. She never asks for passwords, PINs, OTPs or card numbers and never says a payment is verified: only the system's verification result can.
